# Signs of Toxicity

Toxic behaviors or organizations always manifest through weak signals, sometimes accompanied by strong ones. This section lists several of them to help you identify whether you are in a toxic situation yourself.

## Thoughts

Here are recurring thoughts that Allies experienced when subjected to toxic behaviors or organizations.

> If they don’t value me when I’m here, why do they keep me?

> What I do is pointless.

> This whole thing is heading for a wall, yet they act as if there are zero consequences for anyone.

> I’m exhausted just thinking about it.

> I _need_ to leave.

> I’ve had enough. Enough is enough.

> I no longer understand how I ended up working with this person.

> When I ask them what purpose this serves, they don’t answer.

> How do they expect me to do my job properly when they won’t tell me how to do it right? Fine if they don't tell me how, but they should at least give me success indicators. Then I can adapt.

> All of this is truly a waste of time. What did I gain? And what did they gain themselves? Time?

> Anyone can make mistakes. But there is a difference between:
> 
> * making mistakes, taking accountability, and simply changing;
> * and making mistakes, staying quiet, hiding fault when discovered, and blaming others if ever held accountable.
> 
> I cannot work with people who operate like that.

> The grass _may_ be greener on the other side.

> The grass _must_ be greener on the other side.

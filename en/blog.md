## Looking at the Door

<img src="/assets/img/blog/looking-at-the-door.webp" style="border: solid white 1px; border-radius: 12px; max-height: 360px;">

When did waking up early and working harder become a symbol of social success…?

They tell you to work harder to earn more, and you strive to earn more… But why do you want to earn more…? To be happier? But why do you want to be happier? Can’t you just be content with the happiness you already have?

You have 3 units of happiness, or maybe 5, or maybe 7, and you already despise them… That’s your problem. You despise what you own.

In the end, you end up despising yourselves.

Your pseudo-bosses do not want you to earn more or work more; they want you to despise yourselves.

Étienne de la Boétie argued, in _Discourse on Voluntary Servitude_, that tyrants are not defeated by violence, but by withdrawal.

Let just one person prove Étienne wrong! But will you even try to? When tyrants raise their voices a little or threaten you with the professional whip (no raise, termination…), will you cast your eyes down or look toward the door? Will you keep your cool or scramble around them?

If you stopped scrambling and simply watched them while they threatened you, you would realize they never wanted you to react to their threats, but only to stay with them indefinitely.

Here is my advice: the next time a superior, a peer, or a subordinate at your company makes you feel insecure, do not answer. Instead, stare at the exit door until you forget why you are even here.

The Allies is a professional community that values neither people nor work. The Allies values peace (Rule 1 of the [Code of Conduct](/en/code-of-conduct)).
